About

The art market wasn't built for resale.

Selling can take months and fees can climb to 25% or more. Buyers often struggle to verify provenance, and artists rarely benefit when works resell.

Grails is building a better system.

Every artwork carries an Origin‑ID, a record that tracks the work from the artist through every collector who owns it — making resale faster, ownership clear, and artist royalties automatic.
