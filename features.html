Features

Everything you need to collect, hold, and resell art in one place.

01
Artist Royalties
Every resale includes a 4% royalty paid directly to the artist from the platform fee.
02
Zero Seller Fees
Sellers keep 100% of their listing price — buyers pay a platform fee included in the displayed price.
03
Provenance
Established provenance from artist to collector — powered by Origin‑ID for clarity you can see and trust.
04
Verified Resale
List and resell artworks — every sale preserves provenance and royalties.
